# Foothold — /brag plan

**What it is:** An app that breaks any task into tiny steps and shows you just ONE that fits your energy.
**For:** People who freeze at the start, including those with ADHD, low energy, or overwhelm.
**Sets it apart:** It hides the list. One card, a 2-minute first step, "I'm stuck" makes it smaller, and you can fling the card when you're done.
**Most impressive claim:** Zero axe violations, works offline, no account.
**Visual hook:** A pile of 30 overwhelming to-dos that gets swept away, leaving one card.
**Real UI shown:** The deck card, energy picker, capture box, and wins log, rendered with the app's real production CSS and fonts.
**Share caption:** "Your to-do list is the problem. Foothold shows you one tiny step."

**Tone:** default. Punchy and playful, true to the zine look. **Format:** 1920×1080, 30 fps, 20 s.
**Music:** 120 BPM in C major, so one beat = 0.5 s and a bar = 2 s. Kick, offbeat hat, plucked bass, and bell chords (C–Am–F–G). Effects (whoosh, pop, stamp thud) are tuned to the key and land on beats.
